Ethical Considerations & Data Limitations

Provenance

Data provenance

Public series may start at different dates and vary in coverage.

Check series metadata (source, start/end dates) before long-run comparisons. FRED series have individual metadata pages.
Privacy

Privacy limits

Series are aggregates, not individual records.

Aggregates cannot reveal individual accounts; do not infer personal finances from national series.
Bias

Bias & limitations

Data collection methods may bias results.

Survey design, sample frames, and reporting lag can bias series; document these when analyzing sensitive topics.
Responsible Use

Responsible use disclaimer

This dashboard provides aggregate indicators for context — not personal financial advice. Check series metadata, cite sources, and supplement with local research before making decisions.

Avoid over-interpreting short-term noise. If using modelled or imputed series, label them clearly and validate against primary sources before relying on them for policy or financial decisions.
